Cranberry Juice

Description

This is a cranberry juice recipe you can make at home.

Ingredients

  • 4 Cranberry juice cocktail
  • 1/4 White Sugar
  • 3 Whole Cloves
  • 1 Cinnamon Stick
  • 2 Small navel oranges, thinly sliced

Steps

  1. Combine ingredients in a saucepan over medium heat.
  2. Bring to a boil then simmer for 10-20 minutes.
  3. Cool then float one orange slice in each cup
  4. Serve Warm.
