Homemade Hot Chocolate

Description

This is a hot chocolate recipe you can make at home.

Ingredients

  • 3/4c Sugar
  • 1/3c Cocoa
  • 1 pinch Salt
  • 1/3c Water
  • 3 1/2c Milk
  • 3/4tsp Vanilla
  • 1/2c Half-and-half

Steps

  1. Gather ingredients together.
  2. Combine dry ingredients in a saucepan.
  3. Add boiling water and whisk until smooth.
  4. Cook until slightly thickened, about 2 minutes.
  5. Stir in milk until hot but not boiling.
  6. Remove from heat and add vanilla
  7. Add cream.
  8. Serve hot and enjoy!
