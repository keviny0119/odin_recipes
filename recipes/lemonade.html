Lemonade

Description

This is a lemonade recipe you can make at home.

Ingredients

  • 6 Lemons
  • 1c White sugar
  • 6c Water

Steps

  1. Combine ingredients.
  2. Juice lemons.
  3. Combine juice with ingredients in a pitcher and stir until sugar dissolves.
  4. Chill and serve over ice.
